# Data Audit Report

## Dataset coverage
The source files cover BBMP grievance records for 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, and 2024.

## Row counts
- 2020: 91,620
- 2021: 103,504
- 2022: 118,394
- 2023: 119,140
- 2024: 207,016

## Combined dataset size
- Total rows: 639,674
- Unique complaint IDs: 639,674
- Duplicate complaint IDs: 0

## Observed categories
- Electrical
- Solid Waste (Garbage) Related
- Road Maintenance(Engg)
- Forest
- Health Dept
- Veterinary
- Other municipal service categories

## Status distribution
- Closed: 592,468
- Rejected: 16,803
- Non Relevant: 10,932
- Registered: 10,147
- Resolved: 6,953
- ReOpen: 1,043
- In Progress: 896
- Long Term Solution: 412

## Data quality notes
- All key fields were present across the annual datasets.
- Date parsing was required and was performed consistently.
- Missing or malformed status entries were normalized during cleaning.
